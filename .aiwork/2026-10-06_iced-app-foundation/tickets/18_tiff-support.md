# 18 — TIFF support

**What to build:** Decode TIFF (`.tif`, `.tiff`) in core so it shows in the app and works with `pcr`. Currently `big-photo.tiff` in `~/Documents/test-media` (4000×3000, 36 MB) is skipped.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `tiff` feature of the `image` crate enabled; `Format::Tiff`; the catalog lists `.tif`/`.tiff`
- [ ] A small TIFF fixture (generated by `scripts/gen_fixtures.py`), with a decode test in `crates/core/tests/fixtures.rs`
- [ ] EXIF orientation applied, like for JPEG
- [ ] 16-bit and CMYK TIFFs decode or fail with a clear error, without panicking
- [ ] `PC_TRACE` decode time for `big-photo.tiff` recorded
