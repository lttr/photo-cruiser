# Intent

Add a star for picking photos. Keep is super simple: just one star, which marks a photo worth keeping.
It should be saved to the photo's metadata, so it is compatible with and portable to other software like darktable and gThumb.

Background, in the user's words: the primary use is filtering out bad photos, or just picking photos to keep.
